      Method and limits

      Source. The World Bank publishes monthly average prices (nominal US dollars) in the "Monthly Prices" sheet of CMO-Historical-Data-Monthly.xlsx, usually on the second business day of each month. The GitHub version of this page reads a copy that a scheduled job refreshes weekly. The demo page carries a fixed snapshot.

      Change. MoM = (P_t - P_t-1) / |P_t-1| x 100. YoY = (P_t - P_t-12) / |P_t-12| x 100. Both use the latest month with a value. Blank if the earlier month is missing or zero. For spreads and ratios the changes are differences in the spread's own units, not percent.

      12-month z-score. z = (P_t - mean) / sd over the 12 months ending at t, including t, with the sample standard deviation (n - 1). Blank if any of the 12 months is missing or all 12 are equal.

      Percentile. 100 x (count below + 0.5 x count equal) / n over every non-missing month up to and including t. The all-time high of n distinct values scores 100 x (n - 0.5) / n, not 100.

      Spreads and ratios. Month by month: Brent minus WTI; Europe gas (TTF) minus US gas (Henry Hub), and their ratio; urea divided by Europe gas price (mmbtu of gas per tonne of urea, a rough feedstock-margin gauge, not a production cost); gold divided by silver (both per troy ounce).

      Rebase. R_t = P_t / P_base x 100. Series with different units are always shown rebased, so the chart never needs two y-axes.

      Limits.

      • Monthly averages hide intra-month swings. The latest months are often revised in later releases.
      • Nominal dollars, no inflation adjustment. A 1980 price and a 2026 price are not comparable in real terms.
      • Percentile and z-score describe a price against its own loaded history. They are not forecasts or valuation signals, and they change with how much history is loaded.
      • Some series change definition over time (the Pink Sheet notes replacements, for example palm oil from January 2026). The tool does not splice or adjust them.
      • Groups follow the Pink Sheet's sections. Series the tool does not recognize land in Other.
      • Rounding: prices show 0 decimals at 1,000 and above, 1 decimal at 100 and above, else 2. Halves round away from zero.
      • This is not investment advice.
